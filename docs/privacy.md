# Checkers privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026.

Checkers collects no data.

- **No network connection.** The app never connects to the internet or to any server.
- **No permissions.** The app asks for no permission, such as the camera, the microphone,
  contacts or location.
- **No account, no analytics, no tracking.** There is nothing to sign in to, and nothing about
  how you play is measured or sent anywhere.
- **What stays on your iPhone.** The app keeps your saved game, your record against the computer
  and your settings on your iPhone. The app never sends them anywhere, and they are removed when
  you delete the app. Like all app data, they are part of your iPhone's own backups if you have
  backups turned on.
- **Crash reports.** If you choose to share crash reports and diagnostics with app developers in
  your iPhone's settings, Apple collects them under
  [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The app itself collects and
  sends nothing.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
